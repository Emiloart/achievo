# Product Surfaces

## V1 primary surfaces

These are the surfaces that define Achievo v1:

- identity
- organizations
- programs
- milestones
- submissions
- proofs and evidence
- validations and attestations
- verification portal
- profile exports
- admin control plane

## Secondary surfaces

These can remain in the repository, but they are not the primary product story:

- username claim
- privacy controls
- anchoring
- indexer
- monitoring

## Deferred or de-emphasized surfaces

These should not shape the main navigation, roadmap, or architecture unless explicitly requested:

- parties and social/community layers
- username marketplace as a headline product
- quests, streaks, and leaderboard framing
- legacy projection views as user-facing destinations
- risk engine as a front-and-center user feature
- unrelated marketplace or social expansion

## Core hierarchy

Preserve this model when adding or cleaning features:

1. Organization
2. Program
3. Milestone
4. Submission
5. Proof or Evidence
6. Validation or Attestation
7. Export or Artifact
8. Verification

If a change makes this hierarchy harder to understand, it is probably moving the product in the wrong direction.
